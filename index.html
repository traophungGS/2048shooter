How To Play

1

Aim & Fire: Move mouse or drag finger to aim across the 5 columns. Tap/Click or press Space / Up Arrow to launch your tile upwards.

2

Merge Rules: Match equal numbers adjacent (above, below, left, right) to double them (e.g. 4+4=8). Joining 3 at once doubles twice!

3

Chain Combos: Disconnected tiles slide up after merges. Secondary matches trigger multiplier combos (2x, 3x, 4x score!).

4

Ceiling Drop: Every 8 shots, a fresh row pushes down from above. Don't let tiles cross the bottom line!
